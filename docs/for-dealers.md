# Welsh Motorhub — Guide for car dealers

**Who this is for:** showroom owners, sales managers, and staff who list vehicles on Welsh Motorhub.

**What this explains:** what buyers see on the public website, what you control in the dealer portal, and the habits that keep your stock accurate and your leads flowing.

You can share this document with your team. Print it or save as PDF from your editor if needed.

---

## 1. What Welsh Motorhub is

Welsh Motorhub is a **Kenya car marketplace**. Buyers search and compare vehicles from **real dealerships** in one place. We send you **enquiries and phone/WhatsApp contact**; you close the sale at your yard.

**We are not:**

- A broker that negotiates price for you  
- A payment or escrow service (buyers pay you directly)  
- A replacement for your physical showroom  

**We are:**

- Extra visibility for your inventory  
- A structured way to present each car (photos, specs, price)  
- A lead inbox and tools to mark stock as sold when it moves  

---

## 2. What buyers see (the public website)

When someone visits **welshmotorhub.co.ke** (or your linked listing URL), they can:

| Feature | What it means for you |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Search & filters** | Buyers find cars by make, model, price, fuel, body type, location, etc. |
| **Car listing page** | Photos, price, mileage, specs, features, your dealership name, and contact buttons |
| **WhatsApp & call** | Buyer taps to reach **your** number (we do not sit in the middle of the chat) |
| **Enquiry form** | Message comes to you as a **lead** in the portal |
| **Compare** | Buyers can shortlist up to three cars side by side |
| **Your showroom page** | All your **live** stock in one branded page (if your profile is set up) |
| **Import duty tool** | Buyers estimating duty on imports (helps serious import shoppers find you) |

Buyers also see **buyer notes** under many listings: short guidance about that model and that **specific car** (year, body type, fuel). Those notes are based on what **you entered** on the listing, especially **body type** (sedan vs SUV, etc.). Wrong body type on the form means wrong guidance and wrong search filters.

## 3. Your dealer portal

Log in at **welshmotorhub.co.ke/login** with the email your account was created on. You land on the **dashboard**, then use:

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Dashboard** | Overview: views, leads, recent activity |
| **Inventory** | All your cars: add, edit, mark sold, share links |
| **Add car** | Single listing with photos and details |
| **Bulk upload** | Many cars at once via CSV (for larger yards) |
| **Leads** | Enquiries and contact intents from buyers |

| **Plans** | Current access and future pricing (see section 10) |

If you cannot log in, contact Welsh Motorhub support (see section 12). Your admin contact at the platform may need to reset your user or link you to the correct dealership.

## 4. Adding a car (what to get right)

Each listing should reflect **the actual vehicle on your floor**.

**Required habits:**

1. **Photos** — Real photos of **this** car. First photo becomes the main image. Clear exterior, interior, and odometer where possible.  
2. **Price** — The price you want shown (buyers will still negotiate with you directly).  
3. **Year, make, model, variant** — Match the logbook and advertising.  
4. **Body type** — Sedan, SUV, hatchback, pickup, etc. **Must be correct.** Sedans must not be listed as SUVs (and vice versa). This affects search and the buyer story on the page.  
5. **Mileage, fuel, transmission, engine size** — As accurate as possible.  
6. **Description** — Honest condition notes (service history, accident, import grade) build trust.  
7. **Location** — So buyers know where to view the car.

**After you publish:**

- The car appears in **search** and on your **showroom** page.  
- It can appear in **Google** over time via our sitemap and SEO (not instant).  
- Buyers can save, compare, and contact you immediately.

## 5. When a car sells: “Mark sold” and “Mark live”

This is important for buyer trust.

### Mark sold

When the vehicle is sold, reserved, or off the market:

1. Open **Inventory** in the portal.  
2. Tap **Mark sold** on that car.

**What happens:**

| Where | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Search, maps, compare, homepage features | Car **removed immediately** (buyers will not keep enquiring on a gone car) |
| Direct link to that listing | Page stays **up to 7 days** with a **SOLD** notice |
| On the sold page | Buyers see **similar cars** and can still message you to ask what else you have |
| After 7 days | The public listing page is **removed** |

**Why 7 days?** Serious buyers sometimes bookmark a car. The short grace period lets them see it is sold and contact you about **similar stock** instead of thinking your yard is unreliable.

### Mark live

If a sale falls through or you relist the same unit:

- Tap **Mark live** — the car returns to search and your showroom immediately.

**Please mark sold the same day the car leaves your floor.** Ghost listings (sold cars still showing as available) hurt your reputation and ours.

## 6. Leads: what counts and how to respond

A **lead** is typically:

- An enquiry form submission on a listing  
- A tracked WhatsApp or call intent from your listing (where enabled)  

**Your responsibilities:**

- Respond **quickly** (within a few hours on business days; same day is best).  
- Answer honestly about availability, price, viewing times, and logbook.  
- Move the lead status in your process (contacted, viewing booked, sold, etc.) if you use those tools.

Welsh Motorhub **does not** guarantee a minimum number of leads. More accurate listings, good photos, fair pricing, and fast replies improve results.

---

## 7. Your showroom page

Each verified dealership can have a **showroom page** on the site (your name in the dealers directory). It shows:

- Your branding (logo, banner, description)  
- Your **live** inventory only  
- Location and contact details  
- Reviews from buyers (where applicable)  

Keep your **profile** complete in the portal: phone numbers that work, correct county/location, and an up-to-date description. Buyers often decide whether to call based on this page.

---

## 8. Reviews and trust

Buyers may leave **reviews** after dealing with you. Your rating is visible on your profile.

- Reply professionally to fair criticism.  
- Fix real issues (misdescribed cars, no-shows, sold cars still listed).  
- **Verified** status is granted by Welsh Motorhub after checks; it is not automatic for every sign-up.

Misleading listings or repeated complaints can affect your visibility on the platform.

---

## 9. What Welsh Motorhub does not do

| Topic | Position |
|-------|----------|
| **Negotiation** | Between you and the buyer only |
| **Payment** | You collect M-Pesa, bank, or cash; we do not hold money |
| **Registration / NTSA** | You handle transfer paperwork |
| **Inspection** | Unless you offer it; we do not inspect every car |
| **Delivery** | Between you and the buyer |
| **Guarantee** | We list your stock; we do not warrant the vehicle |

## 10. Cost and plans

**Today:** Dealer portal access is **free while we grow traffic together**. There is no in-app checkout or automatic billing on the Plans page until we announce otherwise.

**In the future** we may introduce paid tiers (for example more listings or advanced analytics). You will get **notice by email and in the portal** before any charge.

You **keep 100% of the sale price**; we do not take a commission on the vehicle sale through the platform today.

## 11. Daily checklist for sales staff

- [ ] New arrivals added with correct **body type** and photos  
- [ ] Prices updated when they change  
- [ ] **Mark sold** same day a car leaves the yard  
- [ ] Leads answered within hours  
- [ ] Phone and WhatsApp on listings are staffed  
- [ ] Profile and showroom details still correct

## 12. Bulk upload (larger inventories)

If you use **CSV bulk upload**:

- Use the template provided in the portal  
- Minimum batch sizes may apply  
- Fix errors in the preview before confirming import  
- Each row still needs accurate body type and price  

For one or two cars, **Add car** is usually faster and fewer mistakes.
