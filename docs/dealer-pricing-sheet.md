## Listing plans

Choose the plan that matches how many vehicles you usually have **on the forecourt at one time** (live listings). When a car is sold, mark it sold in the portal — it stops counting toward your cap.

| Plan | Live listings | Per month | Pay 3 months | Pay 6 months | **Free boosts / month** |
|------|---------------|-----------|--------------|--------------|-------------------------|
| **Basic** | Up to **50** | **KSh 3,999** | **KSh 10,999** | **KSh 19,999** | — |
| **Standard** | Up to **150** | **KSh 9,999** | **KSh 27,499** | **KSh 49,999** | **1× Spotlight (7 days)** |
| **Business** | Up to **250** | **KSh 14,999** | **KSh 41,499** | **KSh 74,999** | **2× Spotlight (7 days)** |
| **Enterprise** | Up to **500** | **KSh 22,999** | **KSh 63,499** | **KSh 114,999** | **4× Spotlight (7 days)** + **1× Dealer lift (14 days)** |

**Save on longer plans**

- **3 months:** about **8% off** vs paying month by month  
- **6 months:** about **17% off** vs paying month by month  

*Example: Standard at KSh 9,999/month is KSh 59,994 over six months if paid monthly. Six months upfront is KSh 49,999 — you save KSh 9,995.*

## What is included in every plan

- Your **dealer profile** and **showroom page** on Welsh Motorhub  
- **Inventory portal** — add cars, photos, specs, price; mark sold when stock moves  
- **Leads** — buyer enquiries and WhatsApp/call routing to **your** number  
- Listed on **welshmotorhub.co.ke** search, filters, and maps (as features roll out)  
- **Email support** from our team  

You are responsible for accurate prices, photos, and marking sold cars promptly. Buyers deal with **you** for viewing, negotiation, payment, and transfer.

## Optional visibility boosts

Boosts are **add-ons**. They do not replace a listing plan. Use them when you want a specific car or your whole yard to stand out for a short period.

| Boost | What it does | Price |
|-------|----------------|-------|
| **Hot badge — 7 days** | Visual badge on one listing | **KSh 299** |
| **Spotlight — 7 days** | One vehicle ranked higher in browse and search | **KSh 799** |
| **Spotlight — 30 days** | Same, for 30 days | **KSh 2,499** |
| **Spotlight — 5 pack** | Five separate 7-day spotlights | **KSh 3,499** |
| **Dealer lift — 14 days** | Visibility lift for your whole showroom | **KSh 1,999** |
| **Showroom highlight — 30 days** | Extra prominence on your corridor / showroom pages | **KSh 4,999** |

**Homepage featured dealer** (limited slots, subject to approval): **KSh 29,999 / month**

*Use your plan’s free boosts first each month. Add-ons above are only if you need more.*

## What we do not charge for

- **No commission** on the vehicle sale price  
- **No fee per lead** on the standard plans above  
- **No payment handling** — we do not hold buyer money

## Early access (current)

3. When paid plans start, you choose a tier; **founding dealers** may receive introductory rates (announced by email).

## Terms at a glance

- Prices are in **Kenyan Shillings (KSh)**.  
- **Live listing cap** = cars shown as available; sold units should be marked sold.  
- Plan changes and cancellation policy will be published before billing starts.  
- We may adjust public pricing with **30 days’ notice**; existing prepaid periods stay at the rate paid.  
- VAT treatment will be stated on your invoice when billing goes live.
