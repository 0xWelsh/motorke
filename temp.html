Kenya Car Import FAQs

Does Kenya have car age restrictions?

Kenya does not allow the importation of cars that are beyond eight years so in 2025 your vehicle must not be older than 2018 from first UK registration.

How much does it cost to clear a car from Mombasa?

On top of KRA duties and taxes you will pay Ksh 160,800 as follows:

  • Port charge & SGR Ksh. 120,00
  • Verification fee Ksh. 15,000.00 
  • Interpol Ksh. 15,000.00 
  • Mss levy Ksh. 2,000.00 
  • Radiation Ksh. 1,000.00 
  • Ntsa sticker Ksh. 800 
  • Idf fee Ksh. 2,000.00 
  • Local transport Ksh. 25,000 
  • Agents fee Ksh. 100,000

You can get a clearing and forwarding quote up to Nairobi here https://carluv.co.uk/clearing-and-forwarding-kenya/

How long does it take to ship a car from UK to Kenya?

Shipping is 5-7 weeks from UK or Japan port to Mombasa port. Departures: RORO depends on schedule, container is normally weekly.

Can I get Kenya car duty exemption?

Duty exemptions are available in certain special circumstances. You may qualify if you are:

  • A person with a disability holding a valid badge,

  • A returning resident importing your own vehicle (or a right-hand drive equivalent if moving from a left-hand drive country),

  • A foreign diplomat or exempted official.

Carluv has experience working with the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) to secure duty-free clearance.

Can I export my LHD car to Kenya?
Kenya does not allow importing of LHD vehicles but allows duty-free importation of an equivalent RHD vehicle for returning residents of a LHD country like USA.
Is it cheaper to import or buy a car in Kenya?

It depends on the car’s value. For low valued mass imported vehicles of below KSH 2 million it is normally cheaper to buy locally in Kenya. However, for high end cars like SUVs and pickups normally priced beyond KSh. 2.5m it can be cheaper to import. There is also the aspect of “foreign used car” which may mean you could get a car in better condition due to better road conditions, rigorous testing and maintenance.

What inspections do you do?

CARLUV mechanics perform a thorough mechanical and body check and a 5 mile road test of all vehicles.

In addition, we ensure the car has current UK Ministry of Transport (MOT) certificate without any major advisory.
